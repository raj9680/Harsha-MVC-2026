1. ViewComponent: A View Component is used to create reusable UI components along with server-side processing logic.
It is useful for rendering dynamic and independent sections of a page such as navigation menus, sidebars, latest posts, shopping carts, etc.

Unlike Partial Views, View Components can contain business logic and interact with models or services before rendering the view.

Note:
1. ViewComponent renders a chunk rather than a whole response.
